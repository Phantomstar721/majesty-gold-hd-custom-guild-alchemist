# Custom Guild: Alchemist Lab

Custom Guild: Alchemist Lab adds a complete three-level guild and a new
Alchemist hero to Majesty Gold HD. The project uses private resources for its
building, hero, interfaces, spells, effects, names, and sounds while preserving
the stock systems that own construction, recruitment, research, shopping,
combat, resurrection, Embassy recruitment, and Outpost recruitment.

## Requirements

- Majesty Gold HD
- Majesty Gold HD: Expanded Building Slots, including its DLL launcher
- A CAM merge generated by Majesty Gold HD CAM Merger when this mod is combined
  with another CAM-based content mod

The Expanded Building Slots runtime supports both the public
MajestyHD.exe 1.5.2.24 and Beta2 MajestyHD.exe 1.5.2.28 profiles. Launching the
ordinary Steam executable bypasses the custom building runtime.

## Alchemy Lab

The Alchemy Lab requires a level 2 Palace, houses up to four Alchemists, and
uses its own recruitment and Brewing panels.

| Level | Construction or upgrade cost | Unlock |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1,400 gold | Recruit Alchemists and research Weapon Oil |
| 2 | 1,800 gold | Research Phoenix Phials |
| 3 | 2,200 gold | Brew Vigor Elixir |

The Lab also provides a stock-style Visitors panel. Heroes can enter it to buy
researched Weapon Oil or Phoenix Phials. Recruitment, research, building
upgrades, and Vigor use have separate stock-derived activity ownership, so
those operations can coexist where Majesty permits them.

## Alchemist hero

Alchemists cost 600 gold and fight in melee with a dagger. Their behavior starts
from the Rogue decision tree, removes stealing and ingredient scavenging, and
uses the Warrior basic melee attack lifecycle. They favor preparation and
profitable equipment while retaining ordinary Marketplace, Blacksmith,
Library, and Wizard Guild shopping.

Their base identity includes 11 Vitality, 18 Intelligence, 20 Artifice,
45 Attack, 35 Parry, 35 Dodge, 6 weapon damage, 22 Greed, 18 Luck, poison
immunity, a 25 percent retreat threshold, and movement between a Warrior and a
Rogue. They engage at Rogue attack distance so they can prepare spells before
closing to melee range.

Alchemists prioritize researched Magic Bazaar preparations in this order:
Speed, Strength, Regeneration, Fire Balm, Shapeshift, and Invisibility. They do
not consume the full kit for every trivial encounter. Combat preparation uses
the stock threat estimate, including current spell confidence, before deciding
whether finite potions are warranted. Their private Shapeshift branch uses a
Giant Spider form.

### Alchemist spells

- Flash Flask, level 3: a 30-second self-buff that grants 10 Dodge, 10 Parry,
  and a 400-point action-rate improvement. Cooldown is 45 seconds.
- Stun Serum, level 5: a thrown flask that uses the stock Vines and Paralytic
  Gaze freeze lifecycle to stop a non-building target for 5 seconds. Cooldown
  is 45 seconds.
- Miasma Mist, level 8: a stationary 175-radius cloud with the stock Meteor
  Storm pulse schedule and a 21-second field duration. Each pulse deals one
  point of damage and refreshes a 10-second debuff that reduces Dodge and Parry
  by 10, slows movement by 100, and slows action rate by 1,000. Cooldown is
  60 seconds.

All three spells use private art and sound presentation. Stun Serum and Miasma
Mist reject buildings and lairs as targets.

## Brewing

### Weapon Oil

Weapon Oil research costs 250 gold. A weapon-using hero can buy one oil for
100 gold while still retaining separate stock Poison and Wizard enchanting
channels.

- Paralytic Oil freezes a struck enemy for 3 seconds on the first application,
  2 seconds on the second, and 1 second on the third. The target then receives
  10 seconds of temporary immunity.
- Transmutation Oil grants 5 gold to its owner on a successful weapon hit.
  Greed determines which oil a visiting hero selects.

Oil and Poison states are exposed in the hero Enchantments panel through the
Expanded Building Slots runtime.

### Phoenix Phial

Phoenix Phial research costs 750 gold. A hero can buy one phial for 1,000 gold.
On death it is consumed and has a 75 percent chance to resurrect the hero at
the death location with 25 percent health. The revived hero then enters the
stock Flee behavior. The phial appears in the hero Items panel.

### Vigor Elixir

Vigor Elixir costs the sovereign 1,500 gold and affects every living hero for
30 seconds. It grants 20 current and maximum hit points, improves movement by
200, and raises self estimation by 1. Its application, timed cleanup, repeat
use, progress bar, and mutual exclusion with Rage of Krolm use a private clone
of the complete stock Rage lifecycle. Vigor and Rage cannot stack.

## Embassy and Outpost support

The stock Embassy automatically discovers completed Alchemy Labs because they
remain ordinary guild buildings with a private member title. Embassy and
Outpost random hero recruitment use a private clone of Northern Expansion's
complete 16-hero selector with Alchemist appended as the 17th result. Paladins
remain available.

The explicit Phantoms Haunt compatibility build appends both Phantom and
Alchemist while retaining Haunt's conditional Paladin exclusion only when a
Haunt has been placed. The Alchemy Lab itself has no Paladin conflict.

## Compatibility design

- Dialog ID: `CGAL`
- Controller base: stock `AP10`
- Building resources: `ALB1`, `ALB2`, and `ALB3`
- Hero resource: `ALC1`
- Name generator: private runtime registration `NM18`
- Dataset: `Any`

The package retains the complete Northern Expansion building-dependency table
and appends the Alchemy Lab rule exactly once. This avoids the historical
failure mode where a Base-derived table removed expansion building support.

## Build profiles

The production wrapper builds `dist\CustomGuildAlchemist` and reuses the
verified approved sprite package:

```powershell
.\scripts\Build-CustomGuildAlchemist.ps1
```

Add explicit compatibility or the retained Siege test fixture when needed:

```powershell
.\scripts\Build-CustomGuildAlchemist.ps1 `
  -IncludePhantomsHauntCompatibility `
  -IncludeSiegeTestFixture
```

Deploy a validated package with:

```powershell
.\scripts\Deploy-CustomGuildAlchemist.ps1
```

The Siege fixture is never included unless requested. It provides a completed
level 3 Lab, researched Oil and Phoenix options, a fully researched level 3
Magic Bazaar, supporting stock guilds, funded level 10 test heroes, and extra
sovereign gold for repeatable testing.

Source art, audio projects, generated media, internal research notes, build
outputs, and deployment snapshots are intentionally excluded from the public
repository. Public releases contain only the compiled mod package needed by
players.
