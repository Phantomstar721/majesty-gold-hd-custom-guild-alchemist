# Custom Guild: Alchemist Lab

Turn monster remains, battlefield experience, and a worrying amount of
glassware into bottled possibility.

Custom Guild: Alchemist Lab adds a complete three-level guild and a new voiced
Alchemist hero class to Majesty Gold HD. Recruit lightly armored field
researchers, collect Monster Reagents, develop weapon oils and resurrection
phials, and unlock powerful sovereign alchemy.

## Required launcher

This mod **requires Majesty Mod Manager**:

https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793024054

After subscribing to both items, open the Mod Manager's Workshop folder, run
`Majesty Mod Manager.exe`, select Custom Guild: Alchemist Lab, choose **Prepare
Selected Mods**, and use **Launch Majesty** inside the manager.

Do not enable this package directly in Majesty's mod selector and do not launch
the prepared mod with the ordinary Steam executable. The stock executable does
not initialize the private building and interface runtime used by the Lab.

To combine the Alchemist Lab with Custom Guild: Phantoms Haunt, select both in
the Mod Manager. It creates one compatible local profile. Do not enable the two
raw Workshop packages together without the manager.

## Features

- An original three-level Alchemy Lab with construction, inactive, active,
  damaged, destroyed, and collapsed art
- A new Alchemist hero with complete animation, original portrait, names,
  voiced reactions, equipment priorities, and field-research behavior
- Flash Flask, Stun Serum, and the large lingering Miasma Mist combat cloud
- Monster Reagents gathered from ordinary enemy deaths and deposited at the
  Alchemist's home Lab
- A live Brewing panel with stored Reagent count and a purple pool indicator
  when a level-3 Lab can afford a major recipe
- Weapon Oil with Paralytic and gold-producing Transmutation variants
- Phoenix Phials that give prepared heroes a 75-percent chance to rise again
- Vigor Elixir, a kingdom-wide 30-second hero enhancement costing one Reagent
- Arcane Infusion, a once-per-kingdom permanent +25 Magic Resistance upgrade
  for all current and future Alchemists
- Philosopher's Stone, which converts ten Reagents and one ordinary enemy into
  3,000 kingdom gold
- Stock-shaped Visitors, Embassy, Outpost, research, upgrade, shopping,
  resurrection, progress, and cleanup behavior
- Support for Original Majesty and Northern Expansion content

The Alchemy Lab requires a level-2 Palace and houses four Alchemists. Weapon
Oil research gates the level-2 upgrade, Phoenix Phial research gates level 3,
and locked Brewing discoveries remain hidden until their building level is
available.

Alchemists cost 600 gold and receive a small starting research grant. They
fight with daggers, prepare through ordinary shops and the Magic Bazaar, and
balance nearby resupply against distant reward flags. Each carries up to three
Reagents and strongly prefers returning home with a full load.

The package was built by cloning Majesty's native guild, research, item,
effector, spell, and AI lifecycles into private resources. It appends original
world art, interface art, spell effects, audio, text, and GPL behavior without
replacing a retail guild. The Lab uses private Mod Manager building and Brewing
controllers, a native CAM art package, stock palette-control shadows, and the
larger stock Temple to Krypta blocking footprint.
