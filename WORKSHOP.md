# Custom Guild: Alchemist Lab

Turn monster parts, battlefield experience, and a worrying amount of glassware
into bottled possibility.

The Alchemy Lab is a new three-level guild for Majesty Gold HD. Recruit
Alchemists, dagger-wielding field researchers who prepare for danger with
potions, weapon oils, and thrown mixtures before closing into melee combat.

Features:

- A fully custom three-level Alchemy Lab with original building art, panels,
  sounds, names, and help text
- A new Alchemist hero with complete directional animation and voiced reactions
- Flash Flask, Stun Serum, and the lingering Miasma Mist combat cloud
- Weapon Oil research with Paralytic and gold-producing Transmutation variants
- Phoenix Phials that give prepared heroes a 75 percent chance to rise again
- Vigor Elixir, a kingdom-wide hero brew with a stock-style timed progress bar
- Magic Bazaar shopping and intelligent combat-potion preparation
- Visitors, Embassy, and Outpost support
- Compatibility with Custom Guild: Phantoms Haunt through a merged CAM setup

The Alchemy Lab requires a level 2 Palace. It supports four guild members and
unlocks its strongest discoveries as the building is upgraded.

Requirements:

- Majesty Gold HD
- Majesty Gold HD: Expanded Building Slots
- A merged CAM setup when used alongside another CAM-based content mod

Use the launcher supplied with Expanded Building Slots. Starting Majesty from
the ordinary Steam executable bypasses custom building support.
